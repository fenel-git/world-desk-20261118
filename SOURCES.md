## Limitations

That brief was replaced using another distinct event in the already cached Reuters dispatch, correcting the mismatch without new news research. Some archive pages are empty, and several sites block full-page access; provenance follows the saved source ledger. Treat short cached results accordingly, not as independent full-article re-verification.
